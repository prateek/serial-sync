# Security reporting

Do not post credentials, cookies, session bundles, TOTP seeds, private URLs, paid
content or uninspected support bundles in public issues. Follow the
[safe-sharing checklist](docs/observability.md#safe-sharing) for ordinary bugs.

A private security reporting channel has not yet been established in this
repository's documented policy. Until the maintainer publishes one, withhold
sensitive exploit details and private evidence. A public issue may request a
private contact without describing the vulnerability. Do not assume public issue
attachments or an invented email address are private reporting channels.
