# Keep curated publication metadata portable

Serial-sync owns curated profiles, artwork, descriptions, and overrides, while the reading application owns reading progress. Published EPUBs carry embedded metadata/artwork and available author information in an About page; supplementary reader-specific enrichment is reproducible from retained choices. This avoids losing curation when changing library servers, at the cost of maintaining metadata selection and publication in serial-sync.

Configuration and offline dump/preview are the editing surface. New publications use current choices; previously published copies change through explicit preview/rebuild. The [reader experience plan](../specs/reader-experience.md) defines acceptance. Its [validation record](../research/reader-experience-validation.md) records the selection of stable copies with a narrow BookOrbit navigation patch.
