At the research baseline, the repository had these relevant behaviors:

- Completed configured groups become volumes; open ranges and author books without endpoints remain singles. Frozen completed editions require explicit rebuild for changes. Book identity does not establish complete coverage. See [volume planning](../../internal/app/volumes.go), [rebuild](../../internal/app/rebuild.go), and [configuration](../config.md#volume-output).
- Volume navigation has one entry per release. A multi-chapter post is not automatically split into chapter entries; member navigation documents are omitted and member resource paths use ordinal positions. See [volume construction](../../internal/artifact/volume.go).
- Publication metadata currently supplies title, author, series, position, and date. EPUB attachments without preface transformation pass through unchanged, including in EPUB mode. Cover/description/profile enrichment is not uniformly implemented. See [metadata](../../internal/artifact/metadata.go), [output selection](../../internal/artifact/output.go), and [materialization](../../internal/artifact/materialize.go).
- Content hashing excludes identity enrichment, which is bound to its capture. Existing artifact reuse does not compare enrichment, and volume-edition hashing uses configuration/member content hashes rather than all resolved metadata/assets. Adding portable metadata therefore needs deliberate publication-input comparison and snapshots. Ordinary sync can rematerialize existing non-legacy singles when relevant config changes. See [sync/hashing](../../internal/app/service.go), [capture binding](../../internal/app/enrichment.go), [artifact comparison](../../internal/app/rebuild.go), and [edition planning](../../internal/app/volumes.go).
- Patreon post-detail capture retains raw campaign/user/collection attributes; normalization mainly retains identities and names. Offline enrichment currently uses collection identity/name. Images referenced by profiles need separate capture. See [capture](../../internal/provider/patreon/live.go), [normalization](../../internal/provider/patreon/patreon.go), and [offline enrichment](../../internal/provider/patreon/enrichment.go).
- Hourly scheduling and retryable publication are existing building blocks. Version 2 hooks deliver per-artifact publish/supersede events at least once and require downstream deduplication by event ID. They do not establish a library-ready batch event or a built-in reader adapter. See [hooks](../hooks.md) and [replacement handling](../../internal/app/replacement.go).

The earlier recovered research inspected BookOrbit v2.10.0. The new source audit instead pinned [BookOrbit v3.0.0](https://github.com/bookorbit/bookorbit/releases/tag/v3.0.0) and [Grimmory v3.5.0](https://github.com/grimmory-tools/grimmory/releases/tag/v3.5.0). Both releases were published on September 21. Recheck versions at trial time rather than silently combining evidence from different releases.

Both projects document browser reading and OPDS. Those claims establish candidates for the access requirement, not tested mobile usability or shared progress in arbitrary OPDS clients. See [BookOrbit OPDS](https://bookorbit.app/opds) and [Grimmory OPDS](https://grimmory.org/docs/integration/opds/).

The inspected EPUB views lack the cross-book controls found in their comic readers. BookOrbit's next-series hook is used by its CBX view; Grimmory's EPUB footer moves through sections of the open book. A series field does not establish bidirectional navigation between separate chapter EPUBs. See [BookOrbit EPUB view](https://github.com/bookorbit/bookorbit/blob/v3.0.0/client/src/features/reader/ReaderView.vue), [comic view](https://github.com/bookorbit/bookorbit/blob/v3.0.0/client/src/features/reader/cbz/CbzReaderView.vue), and [Grimmory EPUB footer](https://github.com/grimmory-tools/grimmory/blob/v3.5.0/frontend/src/app/features/readers/ebook-reader/layout/footer/footer.component.html).

EPUB supports embedded descriptive metadata, series membership/order, cover art, and a navigation document. Author biography and portrait can be ordinary internal content. These provisions do not require a library to populate a separate author-profile page. See [EPUB metadata](https://www.w3.org/TR/epub-33/#sec-pkg-metadata), [collection properties](https://www.w3.org/TR/epub-33/#sec-property-belongs-to-collection), and [navigation](https://www.w3.org/TR/epub-33/#sec-nav-toc).

Both pinned importers parse title, author, description, language, series/position, and embedded cover art. Neither inspected importer maps an About page into separate author biographies/portraits. Parser support still needs import/refresh validation under the selected configuration. See [BookOrbit OPF parser](https://github.com/bookorbit/bookorbit/blob/v3.0.0/server/src/modules/metadata/lib/opf-parser.ts), [EPUB extractor](https://github.com/bookorbit/bookorbit/blob/v3.0.0/server/src/modules/metadata/extractors/epub-format.extractor.ts), and [Grimmory extractor](https://github.com/grimmory-tools/grimmory/blob/v3.5.0/backend/src/main/java/org/booklore/service/metadata/extractor/EpubMetadataExtractor.java).

Both expose author-update and image-upload APIs. Separately editable series artwork remains uncertain: BookOrbit's inspected series schema lacks description/artwork fields, and Grimmory's documented series cards use member-book covers. See [BookOrbit authors](https://github.com/bookorbit/bookorbit/blob/v3.0.0/server/src/modules/authors/authors.controller.ts), [Grimmory authors](https://github.com/grimmory-tools/grimmory/blob/v3.5.0/backend/src/main/java/org/booklore/controller/AuthorController.java), [BookOrbit series schema](https://github.com/bookorbit/bookorbit/blob/v3.0.0/server/src/db/schema/series.ts), and [Grimmory series presentation](https://grimmory.org/docs/series/).

BookOrbit saves CFI plus percentage against a file ID and resumes using the locator with a percentage fallback. Its same-path scanner branch updates the existing file row. Grimmory resumes from CFI, then chapter-relative location, then percentage; its explicit replacement method does not itself remap/reset progress. Keeping existing document structure and chapter paths stable is a useful prototype hypothesis, not proof that arbitrary EPUB replacement preserves position. See [BookOrbit resume](https://github.com/bookorbit/bookorbit/blob/v3.0.0/client/src/lib/reading-checkpoint.ts#L22), [scanner](https://github.com/bookorbit/bookorbit/blob/v3.0.0/server/src/modules/scanner/scanner.service.ts#L2248), [Grimmory resume](https://github.com/grimmory-tools/grimmory/blob/v3.5.0/frontend/src/app/features/readers/ebook-reader/ebook-reader.component.ts#L398), and [replacement](https://github.com/grimmory-tools/grimmory/blob/v3.5.0/backend/src/main/java/org/booklore/service/book/BookService.java#L371).

Inspected reader notifications establish in-app events rather than generic phone push for ordinary chapter imports. See [BookOrbit notification types](https://github.com/bookorbit/bookorbit/blob/v3.0.0/packages/types/src/notification.ts) and [Grimmory notification service](https://github.com/grimmory-tools/grimmory/blob/v3.5.0/backend/src/main/java/org/booklore/service/NotificationService.java).

exe.dev's [official iOS announcement](https://blog.exe.dev/ios) explicitly allows VM software to send phone notifications through `notify.int.exe.xyz`; [Shelley's gateway source](https://github.com/boldsoftware/shelley/blob/10f4ce93795cc75841fcba02da042414155ceffe/server/notification_channels.go#L16-L26) confirms the endpoint. The arbitrary-message contract, integration attachment, reader-link behavior, and service limits still need verification. Reusing an existing subscription is the intended first trial; no separate free push allowance has been established.

Hosted ntfy is the free fallback candidate, with [HTTP publishing/clickable links](https://docs.ntfy.sh/publish/) and a [native iOS app](https://docs.ntfy.sh/subscribe/phone/). Free topics are accessible to anyone who knows their names. Use an unguessable topic and minimal summaries, with ordinary reader URLs requiring login; do not send chapter bodies or credential-bearing links. See [topic behavior](https://docs.ntfy.sh/publish/#picking-a-topic) and [privacy](https://docs.ntfy.sh/privacy/). No notification was sent and no integration was deployed during this research.
